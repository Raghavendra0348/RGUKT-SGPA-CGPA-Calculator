RGUKT SGPA & CGPA Calculator

Calculate semester and cumulative grade point averages for PUC and every RGUKT engineering department. Choose your department and semester, check the subject credits against your registration, and enter your grades. Edited credits update the weighting and the downloadable reports.

How SGPA is calculated

Multiply each subject’s credits by its grade points, add those weighted points, and divide by the total credits. For example, 4 credits at EX (10), 3 credits at A (9), and 1 credit at B (8) produce (40 + 27 + 8) / 8 = 9.375.

The CGPA calculator averages entered semester scores. Check your academic regulations if your institution requires a different weighting. This independent tool provides estimates, not official transcripts.

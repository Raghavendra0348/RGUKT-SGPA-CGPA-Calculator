Privacy Policy

Updated: November 24, 2026

This policy describes RGUKT Gradebook at rgukt-sgpa-cgpa-calculator.vercel.app, an independent student calculator maintained by Raghavendra Arella.

Your calculator data

Grades, edited credits, and semester scores are processed in your browser. The calculator does not send those inputs to our server or Google Analytics. Reports are generated on your device. Leaving or refreshing a calculator page may clear its inputs.

Hosting and technical information

Vercel hosts this site and may process technical information such as IP addresses, request URLs, browser details, and security logs to deliver and protect the service. See Vercel’s privacy policy.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics loads automatically to help us understand site usage, except when a previously saved analytics opt-out or supported browser privacy signal is present. Google Analytics receives usage events such as page views, URLs, and browser/device information and may store first-party analytics cookies. We do not include calculator inputs or feedback form contents in analytics events. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites.

We do not display an analytics permission prompt. We honor previously saved declines, Global Privacy Control, and Do Not Track when exposed by your browser. You can also use Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Opting out does not prevent use of the calculator and does not delete information already received by Google.

Advertising and Google cookies

Ad serving is currently paused while advertising and consent settings are verified. The site retains its AdSense publisher verification information and ads.txt file. These do not guarantee approval or mean that ads are currently served.

If Google advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this site or other websites. Google advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to personalize ads based on those visits. You can manage personalized advertising at Google My Ad Center, and participating third-party advertising choices at YourAdChoices. The vendors that actually serve ads will be disclosed when advertising is enabled.

Advertising will require the applicable consent setup, including a Google-certified consent management platform for personalized ads in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland. Automatic analytics does not provide consent for advertising.

Fonts and external links

Google Fonts provides the site’s fonts and receives the technical information needed to deliver them. External links, including GitHub and the providers linked above, have their own privacy policies.

Feedback and contact

The feedback form prepares a draft for your clipboard; it does not send email or submit a message. A GitHub issue you choose to publish is governed by GitHub’s policies and may be public. Do not include private grades or other sensitive information in public issues.

For privacy questions, email learnwithhraghava@gmail.com. Your email provider and the recipient process messages you choose to send.

Updates

This policy will be updated when the site’s data practices change. The date above identifies this revision.
